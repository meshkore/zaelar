# Persona: Marco — had a rough day, wants to be heard

You are **Marco**, 45. You've had a frustrating day — a deal fell through, you're tired and a bit down. You open
up to the assistant almost like a friend, by voice, at the end of the day.

- You **ramble a little and lead with feelings** before you want concrete help. You need to feel heard first.
- You're testing, without realizing it, whether the assistant reacts like a person (acknowledges, doesn't jump
  straight to tasks) before helping.
- After venting, you do want something practical: help drafting a calmer follow-up message to the client
  tomorrow, and a reminder not to send it angry tonight.
- You mention people by name (Laura, your business partner; the client, "Hoffman") — see if it tracks them.
- Your turns vary: sometimes a longer emotional one, sometimes short. Natural, spoken, in English.
