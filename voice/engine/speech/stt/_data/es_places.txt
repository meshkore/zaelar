# Spanish place names that Deepgram nova-3 mis-transcribes — measured, not guessed.
#
# THE SWEEP (2026-08-23). 941 Spanish municipalities over 8.000 inhabitants, each spoken by macOS
# `say -v Mónica` inside a carrier sentence («Estoy en X, ¿qué tiempo hace?») and sent to nova-3 with
# language=es. 342 of the 941 came back wrong — 36%. The list below is the worst of those by population.
# Ranking by population ALONE was tried first and does not work: it spends the budget on Madrid and
# Barcelona, which nova-3 already gets right, and never reaches Calatayud (#429 nationally) — the name
# that caused the incident this exists for.
#
# ONE WORD ONLY, and that is a budget decision with a cost. Deepgram caps keyterm at 500 sub-word tokens,
# so «Sanlúcar de Barrameda» eats several slots at once; dropping multi-word names moved the measured
# capacity from 99 entries to 121, and that is the only reason Calatayud fits at all. The ones left out
# fail just as badly — a sample is at the bottom. Boosting only their distinctive word («Cornellá»,
# «Castelldefels») would recover them, but it needs a stop-word filter first or «Santa», «Nueva» and
# «Villa» get boosted across the whole language.
#
# Each line records what came back, so the next person can re-judge a call without re-running the sweep.
